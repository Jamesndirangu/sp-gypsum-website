Terms & Conditions

Please read and understand the following terms governing all projects undertaken by SP Interior.

1. Payment Terms

The client shall make payments according to the following schedule:

  • 60% Deposit Payment – Payable on the first or second day after commencement of the project. This payment facilitates material procurement, labor mobilization, and project setup.
  • 30% Progress Payment – Payable upon commencement of the ceiling boarding/covering stage (or as agreed based on project progress). This payment supports continued labor and project execution until completion.
  • 10% Final Payment – Payable immediately upon completion of the agreed works and project handover.

2. Scope of Gypsum Works

SP Interior shall provide sufficient skilled and experienced manpower to ensure professional execution of all gypsum ceiling and decorative works according to the agreed design and specifications.

3. Additional Works

Any additional works not included in the initial quotation shall be treated as separate items and may attract additional charges.

4. Electrical, Wiring & Lighting Installations

Discussions, quotations, and agreements regarding electrical wiring, lighting fixtures, and decorative lighting installations shall be conducted after completion of the gypsum ceiling structure unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Materials and labor charges for electrical and lighting works shall be quoted separately from the gypsum works.

5. Project Variations

Any changes requested by the client after work has commenced may affect the project cost, timeline, and material requirements and shall be subject to a revised quotation.

6. Project Completion & Handover

The project shall be considered complete upon inspection and acceptance by the client. The remaining balance becomes due immediately upon completion and handover of the works.

7. Delayed Payments

Failure to make payments as agreed may result in temporary suspension of works until the outstanding balance is settled.
